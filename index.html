    About the lab

    Extending human potential

    The Augmented Human Lab at the National University of Singapore designs interfaces where technology becomes a seamless extension of the human body, mind and identity rather than a separate tool.

    Through Assistive Augmentations we work with people constrained by impairments to live more independently, while extending perceptual, cognitive and creative capabilities for everyone.
